About EliEats

EliEats is a restaurant discovery web application designed to help people find restaurants that better match their dietary preferences and accessibility needs.

Choosing where to eat can be challenging when someone has specific dietary requirements or needs additional accessibility information. EliEats brings restaurant information together in one place to make that process easier.

The goal is to make discovering restaurants more convenient, informative, and inclusive.

Our Purpose

EliEats focuses on making restaurant information easier to explore. Users can search for restaurants, filter available options, view restaurant details, and share their dining experiences.

The project also explores how web development and machine learning can be used to organize restaurant information and analyze customer feedback.
