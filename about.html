About EliEats

EliEats is a restaurant discovery web application designed to help people find restaurants that better match their dietary preferences and accessibility needs.

Choosing where to eat can be challenging when someone has specific dietary requirements or needs additional accessibility information. EliEats brings restaurant information together in one place to make that process easier.

The goal is to make discovering restaurants more convenient, informative, and inclusive.

Our Purpose

EliEats focuses on making restaurant information easier to explore. Users can search for restaurants, filter available options, view restaurant details, and share their dining experiences.

The project also explores how web development and machine learning can be used to organize restaurant information and analyze customer feedback.

Why I Created EliEats

I created EliEats because I realized that people can develop new allergies or become disabled at any time. My step-father fell off a ladder at work and needed to use a wheelchair for almost a year. It was hard to get around and have family nights afterwards since some entrances were in the back of the building. These are situations that do not discriminate, and it is up to the community to help each other.

So I wanted to create something that could help people discover new restaurants while making it easier to find information about dietary options and accessibility.

Through this project, I also wanted to apply what I have learned about web development, databases, and programming to build an application that has a practical purpose.

My goal is to continue improving EliEats and make it a helpful resource for people looking for their next dining experience.
