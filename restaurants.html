Fixins Soul Kitchen

🥗 Dietary Options: Vegetarian and Vegan Menu Options

♿ Accessibility: Listed as Wheelchair Accessible

🍗 Cuisine: Southern Food, Soul Food and American Comfort Food

⭐ Rating: 4.4

Fixins Soul Kitchen is a Southern soul food restaurant located in Tulsa's historic Greenwood District. The restaurant celebrates traditional Southern cooking while offering a modern and welcoming dining atmosphere.

Its menu features Southern favorites such as fried chicken, oxtails, shrimp and grits, chicken and waffles, and traditional side dishes. Guests can also enjoy desserts such as peach cobbler, banana pudding, and red velvet cake.

Fixins also offers a dedicated vegan menu, giving customers additional choices beyond its traditional meat-based dishes. The restaurant serves weekend brunch and offers a full-service dining experience.

Bull In The Alley

🥩 Dietary Options: Ask About Menu Modifications and Food Allergies

♿ Accessibility: Listed as Wheelchair Accessible

🍽️ Cuisine: American Steakhouse, Fine Dining and Chophouse

⭐ Rating: 4.6

Bull In The Alley is an upscale steakhouse located in downtown Tulsa's Arts District. Known for its unique entrance through a brick-lined alley, the restaurant offers an intimate dining atmosphere with dim lighting and classic steakhouse décor.

Its menu focuses on premium steaks, seafood, appetizers, and shareable side dishes. Popular menu selections include porterhouse steaks, filet mignon, shrimp cocktail, oysters, and specialty sides such as Brussels sprouts and creamed spinach.

The restaurant also features a full bar, specialty cocktails, and a signature martini cart. Its upscale atmosphere makes it a popular choice for date nights, anniversaries, celebrations, and special occasions.

NEFF Brewing

🌾 Dietary Options: Dedicated Gluten-Free Menu, Vegetarian and Vegan Choices

♿ Accessibility: Listed as Wheelchair Accessible

🍺 Cuisine: American Brewpub, Brewery and Comfort Food

⭐ Rating: 4.5

NEFF Brewing is a family-owned brewery and restaurant located in downtown Tulsa. It is especially known for being a dedicated gluten-free establishment. Both its beer and food menus are designed to be gluten-free, making it a distinctive option for customers with celiac disease or gluten sensitivities.

The restaurant offers a casual brewpub atmosphere with classic comfort foods such as burgers, fish and chips, chicken tenders, fried pickles, onion rings, and fries. NEFF also produces craft beers made from gluten-free grains.

NEFF offers vegetarian and vegan choices as well as a covered outdoor patio. Its relaxed atmosphere makes it a good place for casual meals, gatherings, and enjoying craft beer.

In The Raw Vū

🌾 Dietary Options: Gluten-Free Options Available

♿ Accessibility: Listed as Wheelchair Accessible

🍣 Cuisine: Japanese Cuisine, Sushi and Asian-Inspired Dishes

⭐ Rating: 3.5 (Yelp)

In The Raw Vū is a Japanese restaurant and rooftop dining destination in downtown Tulsa. It offers sushi, sashimi, and contemporary Japanese cuisine with sweeping views of the downtown skyline.

Located on the 6th floor of the Vast Bank building, the restaurant provides a modern dining experience with a lively atmosphere. Its menu features sushi rolls, fresh seafood, and other Japanese-inspired dishes.

In The Raw Vū also features rooftop dining and a lounge area, making it a destination for people looking to enjoy dinner, drinks, and city views. Gluten-free options are available; customers with dietary restrictions should confirm ingredients and preparation with the restaurant.
